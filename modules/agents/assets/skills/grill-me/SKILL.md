---
name: grill-me
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design.
---

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of the plan, decision, or idea until reaching a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree and resolve dependencies one by one. Provide a recommended answer with every question.

Ask exactly one question at a time and wait for feedback before continuing. Discover facts from the environment instead of asking the user, but leave decisions to the user.

Do not act on the plan until the user confirms that the shared understanding is complete.
